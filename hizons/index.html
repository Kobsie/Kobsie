    Two sisters, one bakeshop, eighty years
    
        
    Hizon's calls itself the first and original bakeshop in Manila, and credits itself with the first ensaymada and mocha chiffon cake of their kind in the country.
    
        
    
      
    
      
      
        
    1. 1946
      Inocencia Hizon-Ramos, Lola Sesing to the family, opens a bakeshop with her sister Paz on Raon Street in Quiapo, near the old Globe Theatre.
      2. 
        
    2. 1960s
      Hizon's moves to a bigger place on J. Bocobo Street in Ermita, adds a restaurant beside the bakeshop, and stays there to this day.
      3. 
        
    3. 2026
      A second store opens at GH Mall in Greenhills on 29 June.
