Pre-order in three steps

Choose your ham, reserve a pickup day before it fills up, and send your deposit proof with the order. You get your reference right away.

1Choose your ham

Chinese-style cured ham: sweet, smoky and salty, eaten with Adelina's thick sauce. Sold by the kilo, so the store weighs your order and confirms the total.

2Pick your pickup day

Pickup runs from November 1 to December 17. Each day takes a set number of hams, so full days close by themselves.

1955

        
Still run by the Uy family

        
Vicente Uy and Adelina Homo-Uy started Adelina's Ham in 1955. Their children run it today, from the same recipe.

        
The ham is cured Chinese-style: sweet, smoky and salty, with a sauce thick enough to spoon over every slice. To serve it warm, pan-fry a slice in the sauce for about 30 seconds.
