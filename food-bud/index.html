Plan your party

Seven steps. Your plan fills in as you go, and nothing is booked until Food Bud confirms it with you.

  1. What are you celebrating?
  2. How many guests?

    Packages start at ₱63,000 for 100 guests. Adults and kids both count.

  3. Pick a theme

    Any character, game or colour. Food Bud styled a Roblox triple birthday on 20 September 2026.

  4. Choose a package

    See what Diamond includes

  5. When is the party?
  6. Where is the venue?
    Home, clubhouse, restaurant or events place.
  7. Send your booking request
    Where should Food Bud reply?
    Names and ages of the celebrants, colours, food allergies.

    How booking works

    1. Food Bud replies with your quote and the package inclusions.
    2. You pay a deposit to hold the date. Food Bud tells you the amount.
    3. You settle the balance by the due date they give you. Then it is party day.

    Request is ready to send

    Send it to Food Bud in one tap. If the message box opens empty, press Copy request first and paste it.
