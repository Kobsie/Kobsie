How ordering works

  1. Pick trays or packs

    Party trays for the table, or packed meals for your crew or office.

  2. Choose a date

    The form only opens dates that are still before their cut-off.

  3. Send one message

    Your order goes to Food Coma on Messenger with a reference number.

  4. Get your total

    Food Coma confirms the total. Send your proof of payment under the same reference.
